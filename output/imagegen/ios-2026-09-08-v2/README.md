## What changed

The large repeated headings and permanent search field are removed. Inbox and AI Inbox share navigation and list geometry. Sender, subject and time use distinct emphasis. Controls follow a common size and weight. Each screen has one primary action, with secondary actions kept quieter.

The two compose screens represent the same layout with different keyboard states. The reader preserves original email content and allows it to scroll.
