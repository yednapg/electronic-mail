# Visual review — revision 3

The completed outputs are checked against the user's requested placements:

- Inbox header: hamburger left, search right, empty center.
- Inbox footer: compose button at right only.
- Default view: AI Inbox; no top switch or heading.
- Mail rows: one sender line, one subject line, exactly two summary lines; shared alignment and spacing.
- Menu: separator-free mailbox rows; Account and Settings at the bottom.
- Reader top: Back plus the desktop's grid, reply, archive, unread, star and trash controls.
- Reader bottom: Reply, Reply All, Forward only.
- New mail and reply: consistent form columns; formatting grouped left, Send right.
- Keyboard: compose toolbar moves above keyboard with horizontal grouping preserved.

All artifacts are static imagegen mockups. Canvas dimensions and file integrity are checked separately in the export manifest. This review does not certify native touch targets or runtime accessibility.
